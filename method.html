Three Parts of You, Always Communicating

01

The Roots · PhysiologyYour body, energy and nervous system

Perpetual fatigue, tension and worry are not flaws; they are signals of a nervous system that’s learned poor long-term strategies for wellness. It’s almost never the individual’s fault that proper physical health practices haven’t been adopted. And it’s so much more than just “work out, eat and sleep better.”

Draws on: Nervous System Regulation · Neurochemistry · Nutrition · Personal Training

  • Signal-Reading. Learning what the body is saying (a tight jaw, shallow breathing, a restless night) so each signal can be understood, articulated and eased.
  • Alarm-Soothing. Regulation techniques around movement, breathing and action that help the body out of fight-freeze-or-flight. Then using gentle, controlled experiments with sensations like a racing heart, so they read as uncomfortable, not dangerous.
  • Resource-Adjustment. Seeing nutrition, movement, tangible objects and people as resources for stabilizing the nervous system. Then using them in more specific ways for overall regulation.
02

The Trunk · PsychologyYour thoughts, patterns and inner rules

The rules and internal moral demands handed down by culture and upbringing are learned patterns. They’re often subconscious, with no clear words attached, and they quietly shape a lot of decisions. We bring them into the conscious mind to put them into words and weigh their value, or lack thereof.

Draws on: Rational Emotive Behavior Therapy (REBT) · Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) · Stoicism

  • TEF-Ungripping. Thoughts, emotions and feelings are not orders to be blindly obeyed. They can arrive, be noticed and understood, and pass without being obeyed. The man who gets angry and lashes out is no different from a crying child who throws his toy at a wall.
  • Rule-Adjustment. Turning rigid and moralistic demands like “I must do better” into flexible preferences like “It seems beneficial to improve my habits, but it’s not a requirement for my existence.”
  • Prediction-Testing. Small, safe, real-world experiments that check the mind’s predictions against what actually happens. Each result helps the mind predict more accurately next time and helps calm the nervous system.
03

The Crown · PhilosophyYour worth, values and direction

A lot of exhaustion is fed by a belief few people ever question: that worth has to be earned. Once worth stops being on the line and the body is flowing with ease and energy, there’s room for the bigger question: what is all of this for?

Draws on: Naturalism · Existentialism · Buddhism · Attachment Theory · REBT · ACT

  • Origin-Tracing. Finding where the “earn your place” rule was learned, and proving that it’s bullshit.
  • Output-Calibration. Pulling apart who you are from what you produce or achieve. (This specific understanding saved me from killing myself and, ironically, has led me to achieve more.)
  • Direction-Freedom. Uncovering what actually matters to you, as opposed to what was “supposed to matter,” so effort becomes effortless as it goes somewhere meaningful.
