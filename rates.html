Rates & Structure

Waiting four weeks to talk through a problem is like calling the fire department by mail. Here’s something better:

$500= 5 sessions+ daily texting+ experiments
  • 5 one-on-one sessions, 1 hour each, all within 20 days.
  • Daily texting. Share thoughts and wins as they happen, get support in the moment, and never wait until the next session for an answer.
  • Real-life experiments. A small, personalized experiment after each session, tested and refined over text.

Why daily? Steady contact with the Wayfinder gives new ideas the influence they need to take root.

Why within 20 days? All five sessions must be scheduled within a 20‑day window. New behaviours need frequent support to hold.

The 60-day guarantee

Expect real, noticeable change within 60 days. If the daily texting and real-life experiments are done and it still feels like nothing has shifted, a partial refund is available, based on engagement throughout the process.

So far, no one has claimed it.

Personalized to you

No two sessions look the same. Each one is shaped around where you are that day, your beliefs, your body and your thoughts, rather than a script or a set agenda.

Time to finish well

Every session has a 20 minute buffer built in afterward, so the clock is never watched closely. If something important comes up near the end, there is room to see it through.

Support is available

If cost is standing between you and this path, adjusted pricing is available for those facing financial hardship.

Proof of circumstances is required for adjusted pricing, such as:

  • A current student ID and class schedule
  • Recent income documentation
  • Statements for debt or a recent major expense
